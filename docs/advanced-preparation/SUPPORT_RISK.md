# Support-Risk Analysis

Support-risk analysis aggregates bounded geometry evidence into neutral review
reasons: overhang, floating component, bridge, low contact, fragile feature,
resin island, or other. Each region reports severity, confidence, surface area,
percentage of evaluated area, material/profile influence, evidence faces, and
limitations.

The result means that an operator should review the named region under the
selected process context. It is not support generation, placement advice, a
slicer replacement, or a claim that supports are required. No support geometry
is created and no mesh or transform is changed.

Material removal, warping, brittleness, and adhesion labels can influence the
advisory wording or severity. Those values are generic defaults and remain
uncalibrated. Skipped, indeterminate, limited, and failed source checks are
preserved; missing evidence never becomes a passing zero-risk result.
