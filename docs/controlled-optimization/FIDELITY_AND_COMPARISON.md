# Fidelity and Comparison

After an applied operation, the coordinator compares bounded before/after facts including counts, bounds, surface area, volume where reliable, build fit, topology state, objective metrics, risk metrics, and objective score. A new critical regression overrides a score increase.

Geometry-changing comparisons report count deltas, area/volume drift, a bounding-box sampled-distance proxy, normal/silhouette/topology limitations, and a PASS, WARNING, FAIL, INDETERMINATE, or SKIPPED_LIMIT state. The sampled distance is explicitly a proxy; no exact Hausdorff distance is claimed. Missing evidence remains indeterminate or skipped and never becomes an improvement.
