# Objectives and Policy

Objective presets include Balanced FDM, Minimum Supports, Maximum Fidelity, Fit to Printer, Stable Base, Lightweight Preview, Resin Advisory, and Custom. Weights are validated for finite non-negative values, normalized deterministically, and retained with source classification, provenance, enabled state, and limitations.

The versioned safe policy bounds scale change, orientation candidates, translation, base height/volume, decimation, geometric deviation, operation count, checkpoint count, evidence count, and performance. Experimental remesh and decimation are disabled by default. Policy hashes are part of candidate, plan, checkpoint, stale-state, and audit evidence.

These objectives are heuristics over available software evidence. They do not claim globally optimal orientation, exact print time/material use, physical adhesion/strength, or guaranteed printability.
