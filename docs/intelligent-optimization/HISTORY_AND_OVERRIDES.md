# History and Overrides

History is local to the current Blender session unless the user explicitly exports it. It records source identity, strategy fingerprint, objectives, policy, constraints, evaluation, rank, recommendation, preview/execution, comparison, accept/discard, timestamps, and schema/software versions.

Users may inspect the frontier, choose another non-dominated strategy, choose a dominated strategy after a warning, change objectives or constraints and rerun, reject a recommendation, save a custom preset, preview, execute, accept a separate copy, or discard. History is not telemetry, cloud sync, hidden learning, or automatic policy mutation.
